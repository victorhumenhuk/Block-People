# Privacy Policy - Block People & Keywords

**Last updated:** 6 April 2026

## Overview

Block People & Keywords is a Chrome extension that redacts or hides user-specified terms, names, and keywords across websites. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles user data.

## Data Collection

Block People & Keywords does **not** collect, store externally, transmit, or share any user data. Specifically, the extension does not collect:

- Personal information (name, email, address, or any identifiers)
- Browsing history or web activity
- Authentication or financial information
- Location data
- Website content
- Usage analytics or telemetry

## How the Extension Works

The extension injects a content script that scans visible text and media attributes on webpages for terms the user has specified in the popup. Matching content is either redacted (blurred) or hidden depending on user settings. All processing occurs locally within your browser. No page content, blocked terms, or matched results are sent to any server.

## Data Storage

The extension stores the user's list of blocked terms and toggle preferences in Chrome's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your browser and is not synced externally.

## Third Parties

No user data is sold, transferred, or disclosed to any third party.

## Permissions

- **storage:** Used to save the user's blocked terms and display preferences locally on their device.
- **host_permissions (<all_urls>):** Required to inject the content script that scans and filters page content on any webpage the user visits.

These permissions are not used to read, collect, or transmit any personal data.

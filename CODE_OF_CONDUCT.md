# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when something causes harm.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of issues, pull requests, or reviews.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to `victor@thesmios.com`. Reports will be reviewed on a best-effort basis. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block contributors when needed to keep the project usable and respectful.

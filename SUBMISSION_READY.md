### Title

### Summary

### Description

Block People & Keywords helps you remove distracting people, names, phrases, and topics from the pages you browse.

What it blocks:

- Names, public figures, and people you want to avoid.
- Keywords, phrases, and topics across page text.
- Matching posts, comments, search results, videos, images, and cards where supported.

Use cases:

- Avoid spoilers while reading news, forums, or social feeds.
- Mute a public figure without leaving the sites you use.
- Reduce doomscrolling by hiding recurring trigger words.
- Focus during work by removing repeated distractions.
- Hide a name from search results or comment threads.

How it works:

The extension scans the page DOM locally in your browser and watches dynamic pages with a content script. Your block list and preferences are saved in browser extension storage; no servers are involved.

Privacy:

No data leaves the browser. There is no analytics, tracking, remote code, telemetry, or advertising. The extension is open source: https://github.com/victorhumenhuk/Block-People

Support and feedback:

Open a GitHub Issue at https://github.com/victorhumenhuk/Block-People/issues or contact Victor on X: https://x.com/victorhumenhuk
